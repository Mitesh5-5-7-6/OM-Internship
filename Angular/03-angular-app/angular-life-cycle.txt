Summary:
- ngOnChanges: Responds to input property changes.
- ngOnInit: Initialization logic.
- ngDoCheck: Custom change detection.
- ngAfterContentInit/Checked: Projected content hooks.
- ngAfterViewInit/Checked: Component view hooks.
- ngOnDestroy: Cleanup before destruction.
